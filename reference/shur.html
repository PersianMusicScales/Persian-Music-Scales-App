How the application represents this model

The application maps an octave of 1200 cents to 360 degrees. Therefore 100 cents corresponds to 30 degrees, 150 cents to 45 degrees, 200 cents to 60 degrees and 250 cents to 75 degrees.

Musicological limitation

This numerical representation describes the pitch-collection model used by this application. It is not intended as a complete definition of a dastgah, avaz or gusheh. Modal identity can additionally involve melodic hierarchy, finalis, shahed, characteristic phrases, variable tones, gusheh context, modulation and performance practice.
