Sources and Model Provenance

This page distinguishes the app’s implemented numerical model from broader musicological literature.

Primary project documentation

  • Pouya Hosseini, Exploring Persian Music Scales through Interactive Visualizations, documenting the circular visualization concept and numerical scale representations used by the application.
  • Open-source repository, documenting the current analyzer, scale data and software behavior.

Background musicology

  • Hormoz Farhat, The Dastgāh Concept in Persian Music, Cambridge University Press, 1990.
  • Bruno Nettl, The Radif of Persian Music: Studies of Structure and Cultural Context in the Classical Music of Iran, Elephant & Cat, 1987.

Citation guidance

When a claim concerns an exact cent value, angle or interval sequence, cite Persian Music Scales as the implemented model. For broader historical or musicological claims, consult the relevant scholarly literature.

Repository citation metadata is available in CITATION.cff.
